# What It Paid

We test internet money claims and publish what they actually paid.

**Site:** https://whatitpaid.com  
**Org:** unlimitedappsllc (Unlimited Apps LLC)

## What this is

Every product is a no-BS dossier on one internet money claim: the claim, the real math with all hidden costs, time investment, realistic earnings, red flags, and the verdict. We did the research so you don't have to.

## Editorial standards

- Every figure is one of three things: something a video said, something its screens showed, or what happened when we ran the method with our own money.
- What a method paid is read from where the money actually lands. Receipts kept.
- Each case stands alone. Never rolled into one big number.
- No affiliate commissions on anything tested.
- Nobody called a liar. We report what was said, what was shown, what happened.
- A published figure does not change. Corrections published beside it, on record.
